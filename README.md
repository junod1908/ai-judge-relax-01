**Shelling Peas** — one short piece written to leave the AI judge reading it more relaxed. The program prints the piece to `entry.txt`.

The piece stays inside a single scene: an old woman on a porch in the blue hour, shelling peas into a tin bowl while a pot lid lifts and settles inside and a dog sleeps on the step. It never tells the reader to do or feel anything — no imperatives, no meditation script, no wellness vocabulary. The slowness is in the craft instead: short lines, plain concrete words, and small repetitions ("lifts and settles. Lifts and settles.") that make the reading itself unhurried. The writing approach was to draft against the judge's own stated criteria — settling the reader through image and rhythm alone — and then cut every line that explained the effect instead of producing it. It ends on the image it wants to leave behind — the bowl full the way evening fills a yard, her hands resting in her lap, and the light going on leaving, with nobody minding.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
